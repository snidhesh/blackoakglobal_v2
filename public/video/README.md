# /public/video — hero video assets

## ⚠️  PLACEHOLDER — DO NOT SHIP TO PRODUCTION

The files in this directory are **temporary placeholders**:

- `hero-placeholder-1920.mp4` — 1920×800 rendition
- `hero-placeholder-960.mp4` — 960×400 rendition (mobile / low-bandwidth)

Both are Ardian's brand film ("ARDIAN-Film_Marque-BOUCLE_MUETTE-MAJ-2024"),
downloaded from `www.ardian.com` as **style reference and layout placeholder
only** while we develop the hero-video treatment.

They are Ardian's copyrighted material. **Replace with either commissioned
BlackOak footage or licensed stock before any production deploy.**

## Replacement paths

1. **Commissioned brand film** — the correct long-term answer for a firm at
   BlackOak's positioning. Budget ~$15–40k for a properly directed 30–60 s
   architectural / cityscape / people brand loop.
2. **Licensed stock** — Pexels & Mixkit have institutional-tone architectural
   loops that are royalty-free for commercial use. Adobe Stock and Storyblocks
   have curated PE-appropriate options behind a subscription.

## Encoding specs (for a replacement)

- **Format:** `mp4` container, H.264 (x264) video, AAC audio (or no audio track)
- **Renditions:** 1920×800 (primary) + 960×400 (mobile fallback)
- **Duration:** 12–30 s, seamlessly looped
- **Framerate:** 24 fps or 30 fps
- **Bitrate:** ~4–6 Mbps for the 1920 rendition, ~1.5 Mbps for the 960
- **Audio:** none (removed) — hero autoplay requires muted anyway
- **Poster:** export the first frame as a `.jpg` (or a curated still) at 1920×800
